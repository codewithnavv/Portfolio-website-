About me

Hi, I’m Naved, a full stack developer pursuing a BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications) at Noida International University. I like owning a feature end to end: design the screen, build the API, model the data and deploy it. Most of my work is in TypeScript and JavaScript.

Right now I’m building AI-powered frontends as an intern at FlyRank, and my time at IBM taught me to think about security before I write the first line.
